Heritage Red Snapper over Broken Jasmine Rice with Root Vegetable Medley

"A soulful fusion of coastal flavors and earth-grown staples, this dish celebrates the delicate texture of red snapper paired with the nutty aroma of broken jasmine rice and caramelized root vegetables."

20mPrep Time
40mCook Time
MediumDifficulty
4Servings

Nutrition

Per 1 Serving
Calories 485
Protein34g
Calcium110mg
Carbs52g
Vitamin D12mcg
Fat14g
Magnesium95mg

Ingredients

4 Red Snapper fillets, 6 oz (170g) each
1.5 cups (280g) broken jasmine rice
3 cups (710ml) water
1 lb (450g) sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed
8 oz (225g) carrots, sliced into rounds
3 tbsp (45ml) red palm oil or olive oil
1 tsp (2g) dried thyme
1 tsp (2g) garlic powder
0.5 tsp (1g) smoked paprika
Salt and black pepper to taste

Step 1–3

Step 1: Preheat and Prep

Preheat your oven to 400°F (205°C). Peel and dice the sweet potatoes and carrots into uniform 1-inch (2.5cm) pieces.

Step 2: Season the Medley

In a large bowl, toss the sweet potatoes and carrots with 1 tbsp (15ml) of oil, thyme, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.

Step 3: Roast the Vegetables

Spread the vegetables on a baking sheet and roast for 25-30 minutes until tender and slightly caramelized at the edges.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Rinse the Broken Rice

Place the broken jasmine rice in a fine-mesh strainer and rinse under cold water until the water runs clear to remove excess starch.

Step 5: Cook the Rice

In a heavy-bottomed pot, combine the rice with 3 cups (710ml) of water and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, then reduce to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes.

Step 6: Prepare the Snapper

Pat the red snapper fillets dry with paper towels. Season both sides with salt, pepper, and a dusting of smoked paprika.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Sear the Fish

Heat the remaining 2 tbsp (30ml) of oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Place the snapper skin-side down and sear for 4 minutes until crispy.

Step 8: Flip and Finish

Carefully flip the fillets and cook for another 2-3 minutes until the flesh is opaque and flakes easily with a fork.

Step 9: Assemble the Plate

Fluff the rice with a fork. Serve a generous portion of rice topped with the roasted medley and a snapper fillet. Garnish with fresh herbs if desired.

Citrus-Marinated Chicken with Caramelized Onion Jus and Fragrant Rice

"A sophisticated fusion of bright acidity and deep savory notes, this dish pairs tender citrus-infused chicken with a rich, silk-like onion reduction served over a bed of perfectly steamed aromatic rice."

20mPrep Time
40mCook Time
MediumDifficulty
4Servings

Nutrition

Per 1 Serving
Calories 560
Protein34g
Calcium55mg
Carbs62g
Vitamin D0.8mcg
Fat19g

Ingredients

1.5 lbs (680g) boneless chicken thighs
1/2 cup (120ml) fresh orange juice
2 tbsp (30ml) fresh lime juice
2 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
1.5 cups (300g) Jasmine rice
2.5 cups (600ml) low-sodium chicken broth
3 tbsp (45ml) extra virgin olive oil
1 tbsp (15g) honey
1 tsp (5g) kosher salt
1/2 tsp (2g) cracked black pepper
1/2 tsp (2g) ground ginger

Step 1–3

Step 1: Marinate the Chicken

In a large bowl, whisk together the orange juice, lime juice, honey, ginger, and half the salt. Submerge the chicken thighs and let marinate for at least 15 minutes.

Step 2: Rinse the Jasmine Rice

Place 1.5 cups (300g) of rice in a fine-mesh sieve and rinse under cold water until the water runs clear to remove excess starch.

Step 3: Steam the Fragrant Rice

Combine the rinsed rice and 2.5 cups (600ml) of chicken broth in a pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15-18 minutes.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Sear the Chicken

Heat 1 tbsp (15ml) of oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Remove chicken from marinade (reserve liquid) and sear for 5-6 minutes per side until golden brown.

Step 5: Caramelize the Onions

Remove chicken from the pan. Add the remaining oil and the sliced onions. Cook over medium heat for 10-12 minutes, stirring frequently until deep brown and soft.

usa

Step 6: Deglaze the Skillet

Pour the reserved citrus marinade into the skillet with the onions. Use a spatula to scrape up the browned bits from the bottom of the pan to create the jus.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Simmer and Reduce

Return the chicken thighs to the skillet. Simmer uncovered for 5-7 minutes until the sauce thickens into a glossy jus and chicken reaches 165°F (74°C).

Step 8: Fluff the Rice

Once the rice has absorbed all the liquid, remove from heat and let stand for 5 minutes. Use a fork to gently fluff the grains.

Step 9: Plate and Serve

Spoon a generous portion of rice onto plates. Top with a chicken thigh and ladle the caramelized onion jus over the top. Garnish with fresh herbs if desired.

Slow-Braised Beef Brisket in a Velvet Roasted Peanut Reduction

"A luxurious, deep-flavored braise where tender beef brisket meets a rich, velvety sauce made from roasted peanuts and aromatic spices. This dish draws inspiration from the heart of Central African culinary traditions, offering a comforting and savory experience."

20mPrep Time
2h 30mCook Time
MediumDifficulty
4Servings

Nutrition

Per 1 Serving
Calories 580
Protein42g
Calcium65mg
Carbs14g
Vitamin D0.8mcg
Fat38g
Magnesium95mg

Ingredients

2 lbs (900g) Beef brisket, cut into 2-inch cubes
2 tbsp (30ml) Vegetable oil
1 large (150g) Red onion, finely chopped
3 cloves (15g) Garlic, minced
1 tbsp (15g) Fresh ginger, grated
1/2 cup (125g) Natural roasted peanut butter (unsweetened)
1.5 cups (350ml) Low-sodium beef stock
14.5 oz (411g) Canned crushed tomatoes
1 tsp (5g) Smoked paprika
1/2 tsp (2g) Cayenne pepper
Salt and black pepper to taste

Step 1–3

Step 1: Prepare the Brisket

Pat the 2 lbs (900g) of beef brisket dry with paper towels and season generously with salt and pepper.

Step 2: Sear the Meat

Heat 2 tbsp (30ml) of oil in a heavy-bottomed pot over medium-high heat. Sear the beef in batches until a deep brown crust forms on all sides.

Step 3: Sauté Aromatics

Remove the beef and add the 150g (1 large) chopped onion to the pot. Sauté for 5 minutes until translucent and slightly caramelized.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Add Garlic and Ginger

Stir in the 15g (3 cloves) minced garlic and 15g (1 tbsp) grated ginger. Cook for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.

Step 5: Build the Sauce Base

Add the 411g (14.5 oz) crushed tomatoes, smoked paprika, and cayenne. Stir well to combine with the aromatics.

Step 6: Whisk the Peanut Reduction

In a separate bowl, whisk the 125g (1/2 cup) peanut butter with a splash of the beef stock until smooth, then pour it into the pot with the remaining 350ml (1.5 cups) stock.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Slow Braise

Return the beef to the pot. Cover and simmer on low heat for about 2 hours, or until the beef is fork-tender and the sauce has thickened into a velvet reduction.

Step 8: Adjust Consistency

If the sauce is too thick, add a tablespoon of water; if too thin, simmer uncovered for the last 10 minutes to concentrate the flavors.

Step 9: Final Garnish

Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve the brisket hot, drizzled with the velvet peanut reduction and garnished with crushed roasted peanuts if desired.

Charcoal-Kissed Lamb Chops with Piquant Mustard-Onion Emulsion

"A masterclass in fire-driven flavor, these lamb chops are seared over traditional African lump charcoal to achieve a perfect crust, then finished with a sharp, velvety mustard-onion emulsion that cuts through the richness of the meat."

20mPrep Time
25mCook Time
MediumDifficulty
4Servings

Per 1 Serving

Protein38g
Calcium55mg
Carbs7g
Vitamin D0.9mcg

Ingredients

2 lbs (907g) lamb rib chops, trimmed
3 tbsp (45ml) Dijon mustard
1 large (150g) red onion, finely grated
4 cloves (12g) garlic, minced
1/4 cup (60ml) extra virgin olive oil
1 tsp (5g) coarse sea salt
1/2 tsp (2g) cracked black pepper
1 tbsp (15ml) fresh lemon juice
2 sprigs (4g) fresh rosemary, finely chopped

Step 1–3

Step 1: Prepare the Herb Rub

In a small clay bowl, whisk together the olive oil, minced garlic, chopped rosemary, sea salt, and black pepper until well combined.

Step 2: Marinate the Lamb

Coat the 2 lbs (907g) of lamb chops thoroughly with the herb rub, ensuring every side is covered. Let rest at room temperature for 15 minutes.

Step 3: Prepare the Charcoal

Ignite the hardwood charcoal in a traditional grill. Wait until the coals are glowing red and covered with a thin layer of grey ash for high-heat searing.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Sauté the Onion Base

Place a small cast iron pan near the edge of the grill. Add a splash of oil and the grated red onion, cooking until softened and slightly caramelized.

Step 5: Create the Emulsion

Whisk the 3 tbsp (45ml) of mustard and 1 tbsp (15ml) of lemon juice into the onions. Stir vigorously until the sauce thickens and becomes glossy.

Step 6: Sear the Lamb

Place the lamb chops directly over the hottest part of the charcoal. Sear for 3-4 minutes per side to develop a deep, dark crust.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Monitor Temperature

Move the chops to a cooler zone of the grill and continue cooking until the internal temperature reaches 145°F (63°C) for medium-rare.

Step 8: Rest the Meat

Transfer the charred chops to a warm wooden platter. Cover loosely and allow them to rest for 5 minutes to redistribute the juices.

Step 9: Finish and Serve

Arrange the chops on a serving plate and generously drizzle the piquant mustard-onion emulsion over the top while still hot.

Crispy Atlantic Mackerel Pastry Pockets with Spicy Tomato Coulis

"Experience a fusion of coastal Atlantic flavors and Central African spice with these golden, flaky pastry pockets. Succulent flaked mackerel is seasoned with aromatic ginger and garlic, then encased in buttery puff pastry and served alongside a vibrant, fiery tomato coulis that cuts through the richness of the fish."

20mPrep Time
30mCook Time
MediumDifficulty
4Servings

Nutrition

Per 1 Serving
Calories 450
Protein25g
Calcium420mg
Carbs45g
Vitamin D16mcg
Fat18g
Magnesium110mg

Ingredients

12 oz (340g) fresh Atlantic mackerel fillets, deboned and flaked
14 oz (400g) puff pastry sheet, chilled
2 tbsp (30ml) vegetable oil
1 small red onion, finely diced
2 cloves garlic, minced
1 tsp (5g) fresh ginger, grated
1/2 tsp (2.5g) scotch bonnet pepper, seeded and minced
1 cup (240ml) thick tomato purée
1 tsp (5g) smoked paprika
1 large egg, beaten for wash
Salt and black pepper to taste

Step 1–3

Step 1: Prepare the Mackerel

Gently flake 12 oz (340g) of Atlantic mackerel fillets into small pieces, ensuring all fine bones are removed. Set aside in a cool bowl.

Step 2: Sauté Aromatics

Heat 1 tbsp (15ml) of oil in a pan over medium heat. Sauté the diced red onion, half the garlic, and the ginger until softened and fragrant.

Step 3: Create the Filling

Mix the flaked mackerel with the sautéed aromatics. Season with salt, pepper, and half of the smoked paprika. Allow the mixture to cool completely.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Simmer Spicy Coulis

In a small pot, combine the tomato purée, remaining garlic, scotch bonnet, and the rest of the paprika. Simmer for 10 minutes until thickened.

Step 5: Roll and Cut Pastry

On a lightly floured surface, roll out 14 oz (400g) of puff pastry. Cut into 8 equal squares approximately 4 inches (10cm) wide.

Step 6: Assemble the Pockets

Place 2 tbsp (30ml) of the fish filling in the center of 4 pastry squares. Top with the remaining squares.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Seal and Score

Press the edges with a fork to seal the pockets tightly. Use a sharp knife to score two small vents on the top of each pastry.

Step 8: Egg Wash and Bake

Brush the tops generously with the beaten egg. Bake in a preheated oven at 400°F (200°C) for 20 minutes until puffed and deeply golden.

Step 9: Final Plating

Serve the crispy pockets hot, accompanied by a generous spoonful of the spicy tomato coulis on the side.

Poached Sea Bream in a Tangy Lime and Okra Infusion

"Experience the vibrant flavors of Central Africa with this tender sea bream, delicately poached in a zesty lime and okra infusion. This dish harmonizes the silky texture of fresh fish with the natural thickening power of okra and the bright, citrusy punch of lime, creating a healthy and soul-warming meal."

20mPrep Time
25mCook Time
MediumDifficulty
4Servings

Nutrition

Per 1 Serving
Calories 340
Protein38g
Calcium140mg
Carbs12g
Vitamin D12mcg
Fat15g
Magnesium110mg

Ingredients

4 sea bream fillets (about 5 oz (142g) each)
12 oz (340g) fresh okra, sliced into rounds
1/4 cup (60ml) fresh lime juice
1 tbsp (6g) lime zest
2 tbsp (30ml) vegetable oil
1 medium red onion, finely diced
3 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp (6g) fresh ginger, finely grated
2 cups (475ml) fish stock
1 tsp (5g) sea salt
1/2 tsp (1g) black pepper
1 Scotch Bonnet pepper, left whole

Step 1–3

Step 1: Prepare the Fish

Rinse the 4 sea bream fillets (about 5 oz (142g) each) under cold water, pat them dry with paper towels, and season both sides with 1 tsp (5g) of sea salt and 1/2 tsp (1g) of black pepper.

Step 2: Heat the Base

In a heavy-bottomed traditional pot, heat 2 tbsp (30ml) of vegetable oil over medium heat until shimmering.

Step 3: Sauté Aromatics

Add the diced red onion and 3 cloves of minced garlic to the pot, stirring constantly for about 4 minutes until they are soft and translucent.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Infuse Spices

Stir in 1 tbsp (6g) of grated ginger and add the whole Scotch Bonnet pepper; sauté for 1 minute to release the aromatic oils without breaking the pepper.

Step 5: Incorporate Okra

Add the 12 oz (340g) of sliced okra to the pot and sauté for 3 minutes, allowing them to coat in the oil and spices.

Step 6: Simmer the Broth

Pour in 2 cups (475ml) of fish stock and 1/4 cup (60ml) of lime juice; bring the mixture to a gentle simmer over medium-low heat.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Begin Poaching

Carefully lay the sea bream fillets into the simmering broth, ensuring they are nestled among the okra and partially submerged.

Step 8: Gentle Simmering

Reduce the heat to low, cover the pot, and poach the fish for 8 to 10 minutes, or until the fish reaches an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C) and flakes easily.

Step 9: Finish and Serve

Remove the whole pepper, stir in 1 tbsp (6g) of lime zest, and ladle the fish and okra broth into deep bowls for serving.

Black-Eyed Pea Soufflé Fritters with a Piquant Tomato and Chili Coulis

"An elevated interpretation of traditional West and Central African bean cakes, these fritters are whisked to a light, soufflé-like consistency and paired with a sharp, vibrant tomato and Scotch Bonnet coulis."

30mPrep Time
25mCook Time
MediumDifficulty
4Servings

Nutrition

Per 1 Serving
Calories 425
Protein21g
Calcium95mg
Carbs54g
Vitamin D0mcg
Fat16g
Magnesium142mg

Ingredients

2 cups (400g) dried black-eyed peas, soaked overnight
1/2 cup (120ml) water for blending
1 large red onion, finely chopped
1 tsp (6g) sea salt
2 cups (475ml) vegetable oil for frying
3 ripe medium tomatoes, quartered
1 Scotch Bonnet pepper, seeds removed
1 tbsp (15ml) red palm oil or olive oil
2 cloves garlic, minced

Step 1–3

Step 1: Peel the Beans

After soaking 2 cups (400g) of peas, rub them vigorously between your palms to loosen the skins. Rinse with water and pour off the floating skins until the beans are clean.

Step 2: Create the Base Paste

Combine the peeled peas, half of the chopped onion, and 1/2 cup (120ml) water in a blender. Process until the mixture is a thick, perfectly smooth paste.

Step 3: Aerate for Soufflé Texture

Transfer the paste to a large bowl. Using a wooden spoon, whisk the batter in a circular motion for 10 minutes to incorporate air until it doubles in volume and feels light.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Prepare the Coulis Base

In a clean blender, combine the tomatoes, Scotch Bonnet pepper, and garlic. Pulse until you achieve a smooth liquid consistency for the sauce.

Step 5: Simmer the Piquant Sauce

Heat 1 tbsp (15ml) oil in a small pan. Add the remaining onions, sauté until translucent, then pour in the tomato mix. Simmer for 15 minutes until thickened.

Step 6: Heat the Frying Oil

Pour 2 cups (475ml) of vegetable oil into a deep heavy-bottomed pot. Heat until it reaches 350°F (175°C) or until a small drop of batter sizzles immediately.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Fry the Fritters

Carefully drop 1 tbsp (15ml) scoops of the aerated batter into the hot oil. Do not overcrowd the pot; fry in batches to maintain the light texture.

Step 8: Achieve Golden Perfection

Fry each fritter for about 3 minutes, turning once, until they are golden brown on all sides and have puffed up significantly like small soufflés.

Step 9: Drain and Garnish

Remove the fritters and drain them on a wire rack or paper towels. Serve immediately while hot and crispy with a side of the warm chili coulis.

Hand-Rolled Millet Couscous with Braised Lamb and Garden Vegetables

"A soul-warming traditional dish featuring nutrient-dense millet hand-rolled into delicate pearls, served alongside tender lamb slow-braised with vibrant garden vegetables in a fragrant, spiced broth."

30mPrep Time
1h 15mCook Time
MediumDifficulty
4Servings

Nutrition

Per 1 Serving
Calories 540

Calcium65mg
Carbs58g
Vitamin D0.8mcg

Ingredients

1.5 lbs (680g) lamb shoulder, cubed
2 cups (340g) fine millet flour or meal
1/2 cup (120ml) water for rolling
2 tbsp (30ml) vegetable oil
1 large (150g) onion, diced
2 medium (120g) carrots, sliced into rounds
1 large (200g) zucchini, chopped
2 tbsp (30ml) tomato paste
4 cups (950ml) beef or vegetable broth
1 tsp (5g) ground cumin
1 tsp (5g) ground coriander
Salt and black pepper to taste

Step 1–3

Step 1: Prepare the Lamb

Season 1.5 lbs (680g) of cubed lamb shoulder with salt and pepper, ensuring even coverage for optimal browning.

Step 2: Sear the Meat

Heat 2 tbsp (30ml) of oil in a heavy traditional pot; sear the lamb over medium-high heat until a deep golden crust forms on all sides.

Step 3: Sauté Aromatics

Add the diced onion to the pot and cook for 5 minutes until translucent, stirring frequently to incorporate the browned bits from the lamb.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Build the Braising Liquid

Stir in 2 tbsp (30ml) tomato paste, cumin, and coriander. Pour in 4 cups (950ml) of broth and bring to a gentle boil.

Step 5: Hand-Roll the Millet

Place 2 cups (340g) of millet flour in a wide wooden bowl. Gradually spray 1/2 cup (120ml) water while using a circular palm motion to form tiny, uniform pellets.

Step 6: Initial Steam

Place the hand-rolled millet into a steamer basket lined with cheesecloth. Set over the simmering stew and steam for 20 minutes.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Incorporate Vegetables

Carefully remove the steamer. Add the sliced carrots and zucchini to the lamb broth, ensuring they are submerged to cook evenly.

Step 8: Final Steam and Fluff

Return the millet steamer to the pot. Steam for another 15 minutes. Once done, transfer millet to a bowl and fluff with a fork to separate grains.

Step 9: Plating the Feast

Mound the fluffy millet on a large shared platter. Create a well in the center and ladle the tender lamb and vegetables with plenty of aromatic sauce.

Tamarind-Glazed Beef in a Rich Tomato and Carrot Reduction

"A sophisticated Central African-inspired beef stew where the tartness of tamarind meets a sweet, slow-cooked reduction of carrots and tomatoes. The beef is braised until melt-in-your-mouth tender, coated in a glossy, savory-sour glaze."

20mPrep Time
1h 30mCook Time
MediumDifficulty
4Servings

Nutrition

Per 1 Serving
Calories 485
Protein38g
Calcium75mg
Carbs16g
Vitamin D1mcg
Fat28g
Magnesium65mg

Ingredients

1.5 lbs (680g) beef chuck, cut into 1-inch cubes
2 tbsp (30ml) vegetable oil
1 large onion, finely diced
3 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp (15g) fresh ginger, grated
3 large carrots, sliced into 0.5-inch (1.3cm) rounds
4 medium tomatoes, roughly chopped
2 tbsp (30g) tamarind paste concentrate
2 cups (475ml) beef broth
1 tsp (5g) sea salt
0.5 tsp (2g) cracked black pepper
0.25 tsp (1g) cayenne pepper
Fresh cilantro for garnish

Step 1–3

Step 1: Season the Beef

In a large bowl, toss the cubed beef with 1 tsp (5g) salt and 0.5 tsp (2g) black pepper until evenly coated.

Step 2: Sear the Meat

Heat 2 tbsp (30ml) oil in a heavy-bottomed pot over medium-high heat. Sear the beef in batches until browned on all sides, then remove and set aside.

Step 3: Sauté Aromatics

In the same pot, add the diced onion. Cook for 5 minutes until translucent, then stir in the minced garlic and grated ginger for another minute.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Build the Vegetable Base

Add the sliced carrots and chopped tomatoes to the pot. Stir well, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot.

Step 5: Deglaze and Braise

Return the beef to the pot. Pour in 2 cups (475ml) beef broth and add the cayenne pepper. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low.

Step 6: Slow Simmer

Cover the pot with a tight-fitting lid and simmer for 60 to 70 minutes, or until the beef is very tender when pierced with a fork.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Integrate Tamarind

Whisk 2 tbsp (30g) tamarind paste with a small amount of the hot cooking liquid, then stir it back into the pot.

Step 8: Thicken the Reduction

Remove the lid and increase heat slightly. Simmer for another 10 minutes until the sauce reduces into a thick, glossy glaze that coats the carrots and beef.

Step 9: Final Garnish and Serve

Adjust salt to taste. Serve hot in bowls, garnished with fresh cilantro, ideally alongside rice or fufu.

Hand-Wrapped Prawn and Herb Crisps with Sweet Chili Dipping Sauce

"These delicate, hand-wrapped prawn crisps are a fusion of succulent seafood and fresh aromatic herbs, fried to a perfect golden crunch. Served with a vibrant sweet chili dipping sauce, they offer a sophisticated balance of textures and flavors."

25mPrep Time
20mCook Time
MediumDifficulty
4Servings

Nutrition

Per 1 Serving
Calories 450
Protein25g
Calcium420mg
Carbs45g
Vitamin D16mcg
Fat18g
Magnesium110mg

Ingredients

1 lb (454g) large prawns, peeled and deveined
12 sheets (200g) spring roll pastry wrappers
1/2 cup (15g) fresh cilantro, finely chopped
1/4 cup (10g) fresh mint leaves, minced
2 cloves (6g) garlic, minced
1 tsp (5g) fresh ginger, grated
1 tbsp (15ml) light soy sauce
2 cups (475ml) vegetable oil for deep frying
1/2 cup (120ml) sweet chili sauce for dipping

Step 1–3

Step 1: Prepare the Prawn Filling

Finely chop the prawns until they form a coarse paste. Place them in a bowl and mix with the minced garlic, grated ginger, and soy sauce.

Step 2: Incorporate Fresh Herbs

Fold the chopped cilantro and mint into the prawn mixture until evenly distributed. This adds the signature aromatic herbal note.

Step 3: Prepare the Wrappers

Lay out the spring roll pastry sheets on a clean surface. Cut each sheet into smaller rectangles if they are large, approximately 4 inches (10cm) wide.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Portion the Filling

Place 1 tbsp (15g) of the prawn and herb mixture at the bottom center of each pastry rectangle.

Step 5: Fold the Crisps

Fold the bottom edge over the filling, then tuck in the sides and roll tightly to form a small, neat parcel or cigar shape.

Step 6: Seal the Edges

Dab a small amount of water or cornstarch slurry on the top edge of the pastry to seal the roll completely, ensuring no filling can escape.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Heat the Oil

Pour the vegetable oil into a heavy-bottomed pot and heat to 350°F (175°C). The oil should be shimmering but not smoking.

Step 8: Fry Until Golden

Carefully place 3 to 4 rolls into the hot oil at a time. Fry for 3 to 4 minutes, turning occasionally, until they are deep golden brown and crispy.

Step 9: Drain and Serve

Remove the crisps from the oil and drain on a wire rack or paper towels. Serve immediately while hot with 1/2 cup (120ml) of sweet chili dipping sauce.

Steamed Millet Grains in a Sweetened Vanilla and Nutmeg Cream

"This comforting dessert features fluffy, steamed millet grains enveloped in a luxurious, velvet-smooth cream infused with aromatic vanilla and freshly grated nutmeg. A traditional Central African staple reimagined as a sophisticated sweet treat."

45mCook Time

Ingredients

1 cup (200g) hulled millet grains
2 cups (475ml) water for soaking
1.5 cups (355ml) heavy cream
0.33 cup (65g) granulated sugar
1 tbsp (14g) unsalted butter
1 tsp (5ml) pure vanilla extract
0.5 tsp (1g) freshly grated nutmeg
1 pinch (0.5g) sea salt
0.25 cup (30g) crushed toasted peanuts for garnish

Step 1–3

Step 1: Rinse the Grains

Place the millet in a fine-mesh sieve and rinse thoroughly under cold running water until the water runs clear.

Step 2: Soak the Millet

Transfer the millet to a bowl and cover with 2 cups (475ml) of water; let soak for 10 minutes to soften the outer husk.

Step 3: Prepare the Steamer

Fill a large pot with 2 inches (5cm) of water and bring to a boil; line a steamer basket with cheesecloth.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Steam the Grains

Spread the millet evenly in the steamer basket, cover tightly, and steam for 25 minutes until the grains are tender and fluffy.

Step 5: Simmer the Cream

In a separate saucepan, combine the heavy cream and sugar over medium heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves completely.

Step 6: Infuse the Aromatics

Whisk in the vanilla extract, freshly grated nutmeg, and salt; simmer for 5 minutes to let the flavors meld.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Combine Millet and Cream

Gently fold the steamed millet and the butter into the hot cream mixture, ensuring every grain is coated.

Step 8: Thicken the Mixture

Reduce the heat to low and cook for another 5 minutes, allowing the millet to absorb some of the cream until it reaches a porridge-like consistency.

Step 9: Final Garnish and Serve

Ladle the warm millet cream into bowls and top with a sprinkle of extra nutmeg and crushed peanuts.

Wild Hibiscus and Mint Infusion with Spiced Sugar Rim

"A vibrant and refreshing Central African-inspired beverage, this infusion combines the tartness of wild hibiscus petals with cooling mint and warm spices. The glass is finished with a sparkling cinnamon-sugar rim for a sophisticated touch of sweetness."

15mPrep Time
10mCook Time
EasyDifficulty
4Servings

Nutrition

Per 1 Serving
Calories 135

Calcium22mg
Carbs34g
Vitamin D0mcg

Ingredients

1 cup (25g) dried wild hibiscus petals (Bissap)
4 cups (950ml) filtered water
1 oz (30g) fresh mint leaves, plus extra for garnish
1 inch (2.5cm) fresh ginger, peeled and sliced
0.25 tsp (0.5g) ground cloves
0.5 cup (100g) granulated sugar for the infusion
2 tbsp (25g) granulated sugar for the rim
0.5 tsp (1g) ground cinnamon
1 lime, cut into wedges

Step 1–3

Step 1: Prepare Spiced Sugar

On a small, flat plate, combine 2 tbsp (25g) sugar and 0.5 tsp (1g) ground cinnamon, mixing well with a spoon.

Step 2: Boil Water and Spices

In a traditional metal or clay pot, bring 4 cups (950ml) of water to a rolling boil over medium-high heat.

Step 3: Infuse Aromatics

Add the 1 inch (2.5cm) of sliced ginger and 0.25 tsp (0.5g) ground cloves to the boiling water and simmer for 2 minutes.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Add Hibiscus Petals

Stir in 1 cup (25g) of dried hibiscus petals, then immediately remove the pot from the heat source.

Step 5: Incorporate Fresh Mint

Place 1 oz (30g) of fresh mint leaves into the pot, pressing them down with a spoon to submerge them in the hot liquid.

Step 6: Steep the Infusion

Cover the pot with a tight-fitting lid and let the mixture steep for 10 to 12 minutes until the liquid is deep crimson.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Strain the Mixture

Pour the infusion through a fine-mesh sieve into a large heat-proof pitcher, discarding the spent petals, mint, and ginger.

Step 8: Sweeten the Drink

While the liquid is still warm, stir in 0.5 cup (100g) of sugar until it has completely dissolved.

Step 9: Rim and Serve

Rub a lime wedge around the rim of 4 glasses, dip them into the cinnamon sugar, and fill with ice before pouring the hibiscus infusion over.

Velvet Baobab Fruit Nectar with Toasted Coconut and Honeycomb

"This Velvet Baobab Fruit Nectar is a sophisticated, nutrient-dense beverage that combines the tangy, citrus-like profile of African baobab with the creamy richness of high-protein Greek yogurt. Topped with hand-toasted coconut and raw honeycomb, it offers a multi-textured experience that is both refreshing and deeply satisfying for a senior-focused nutritional profile."

5mCook Time
EasyDifficulty
4Servings

Nutrition

Per 1 Serving

Ingredients

4 oz (113g) Baobab fruit powder
3 cups (710ml) Full-fat Greek yogurt
1.5 cups (355ml) Cold coconut water
1/2 cup (45g) Unsweetened shredded coconut
4 tbsp (84g) Fresh raw honeycomb chunks
3 tbsp (45ml) Wildflower honey
1/2 tsp (1g) Ground ginger
1 tsp (5ml) Vanilla bean paste
1/4 tsp (1.5g) Sea salt

Step 1–3

Step 1: Toast the Coconut

Place the 1/2 cup (45g) shredded coconut in a dry skillet over medium heat. Stir constantly for 3 to 5 minutes until golden brown and fragrant, then transfer to a small bowl to cool. 

Step 2: Rehydrate the Baobab

In a large ceramic bowl, whisk the 4 oz (113g) baobab powder with 1/2 cup (118ml) of the coconut water until a smooth, thick paste forms. 

Step 3: Incorporate the Yogurt

Fold the 3 cups (710ml) of Greek yogurt into the baobab paste using a wooden spoon until the color is uniform. 

Step 4–6

Step 4: Thin the Nectar

Slowly pour in the remaining 1 cup (237ml) of coconut water while whisking gently to achieve a velvet-smooth, pourable consistency.

Step 5: Sweeten and Spice

Add the 3 tbsp (45ml) wildflower honey, 1/2 tsp (1g) ground ginger, vanilla bean paste, and sea salt. Whisk vigorously to distribute the flavors.

Step 6: Chill the Mixture

Cover the bowl and place it in the refrigerator for at least 20 minutes to allow the flavors to meld and the nectar to thicken slightly.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Prepare the Honeycomb

Using a sharp knife, carefully slice the 4 tbsp (84g) honeycomb into four equal, beautiful chunks.

Step 8: Final Whisk

Remove from the refrigerator and give the nectar one final brisk whisk to ensure it is perfectly aerated and light. 

Step 9: Assemble and Garnish

Pour the nectar into four glasses. Top each with a generous sprinkle of toasted coconut and place a honeycomb chunk in the center.
