Miso-Glazed Atlantic Salmon with Toasted Sesame Air Fryer Floret

"Imagine the deep, fermented richness of white miso meeting the buttery, melt-in-your-mouth flakes of cold-water Atlantic salmon. This dish balances the savory depth of Japanese umami with the revitalizing crunch of air-fried broccoli florets, specifically designed to support bone density and hormonal harmony. Every bite offers a sophisticated interplay of textures, from the lacquered glaze of the fish to the nutty, toasted sesame finish of the greens."

Prep Time15m
Cook Time12m
DifficultyMedia
Servings4
Nutrition465 kcal • Pro: 36g | Carbs: 14g | Fat: 28g | Ca: 185mg | Vit D: 18mcg | Mag: 95mg

Ingredients

600g (21 oz) Atlantic Salmon Fillets, skin-on
60g (4 tbsp) White Miso Paste
30ml (2 tbsp) Mirin
15ml (1 tbsp) Raw Honey
10g (2 tsp) Fresh Ginger, grated
500g (1.1 lb) Broccoli Florets
20ml (1.3 tbsp) Toasted Sesame Oil
15g (2 tbsp) Toasted Sesame Seeds
5ml (1 tsp) Low-Sodium Soy Sauce

Step 1–3

Step 1: The Umami Emulsion

In a small ceramic bowl, whisk together the white miso paste, mirin, raw honey, and freshly grated ginger until the mixture is completely smooth. This concentrated glaze provides the essential probiotics and deep flavor profile that characterizes the dish. Ensure there are no lumps of miso remaining to guarantee an even coating on the fish.

Step 2: Fillet Architecture

Pat the Atlantic salmon fillets dry using high-quality paper towels to remove all surface moisture, which is the secret to a perfect sear. Slice the salmon into four equal portions of approximately 150g (5.3 oz) each. Place them on a tray and lightly brush only the flesh side with a tiny drop of sesame oil.

Step 3: Vegetable Seasoning

Place the broccoli florets into a large mixing bowl and drizzle with the remaining toasted sesame oil and a splash of soy sauce. Toss vigorously with your hands or tongs to ensure every crevice of the floret is lightly coated. This preparation ensures the broccoli crisps up beautifully in the air fryer without becoming soggy.
