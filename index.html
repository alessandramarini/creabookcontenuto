Truffle-Scented Silken Tofu with Toasted Sesame and Micro-Cilantro

"Imagine a cloud of velvet-soft soy curd, delicately infused with the earthy, musky depths of white truffle and the sharp, nutty crunch of fire-toasted sesame. This dish is a study in textural contrast, where the ethereal lightness of silken tofu meets the vibrant, citrusy punch of micro-cilantro, creating a sophisticated appetizer that dances across the palate with umami elegance."

Prep Time15m
Cook Time10m
DifficultyMedium
Servings4
Nutrition210 kcal • Pro: 14g | Carbs: 10g | Fat: 13g | Ca: 240mg | Vit D: 5mcg | Mag: 75mg

Ingredients

600g (21 oz) Premium Silken Tofu
30ml (2 tbsp) White Truffle Oil
15g (0.5 oz) Toasted White and Black Sesame Seeds
45ml (3 tbsp) Light Soy Sauce
10ml (2 tsp) Toasted Sesame Oil
5g (0.18 oz) Fresh Micro-Cilantro
10g (0.35 oz) Finely Grated Fresh Ginger
15g (0.5 oz) Thinly Sliced Scallions
5ml (1 tsp) Raw Honey

Step 1–3

Step 1: Drain the Tofu

Carefully remove 600g (21 oz) of silken tofu from its packaging and drain the excess liquid by letting it sit on a tilted board for 10 minutes.

Step 2: Toast the Seeds

In a small dry skillet over medium heat, toast 15g (0.5 oz) of sesame seeds for 3 minutes until they release a nutty aroma and turn golden.

Step 3: Prepare the Umami Base

In a small ceramic bowl, whisk together 45ml (3 tbsp) of light soy sauce with 10ml (2 tsp) of honey until fully dissolved.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Infuse the Aromatics

Stir 10g (0.35 oz) of freshly grated ginger and 10ml (2 tsp) of toasted sesame oil into the soy mixture to build the flavor profile.

Step 5: Warm the Tofu

Place the tofu in a steamer basket over boiling water and steam at 100°C (212°F) for exactly 5 minutes to take the chill off without breaking the structure.

Step 6: Slice for Presentation

Using a very sharp damp knife, slice the warm tofu into 4 equal rectangular blocks, approximately 150g (5.3 oz) each.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Drizzle the Truffle Essence

Arrange the tofu blocks on chilled plates and drizzle 7.5ml (0.5 tbsp) of white truffle oil over each portion, ensuring it coats the surface.

Step 8: Apply the Soy Glaze

Spoon the prepared ginger-soy mixture over the center of each tofu block, allowing it to cascade down the sides in thin ribbons.

Step 9: Garnish and Serve

Top each serving with the toasted sesame seeds, sliced scallions, and a generous pinch of fresh micro-cilantro before serving immediately.

Charred Lacinato Kale and Marcona Almond Gremolata

"Embark on a culinary journey where the rugged, obsidian-hued leaves of Lacinato kale are transformed by the fierce kiss of a cast-iron sear, creating a smoky foundation for the buttery crunch of Mediterranean Marcona almonds and a vibrant, citrus-forward gremolata."

Prep Time15m
Cook Time10m
DifficultyMedia
Servings4
Nutrition245 kcal • Pro: 9g | Carbs: 16g | Fat: 22g | Ca: 280mg | Vit D: 1mcg | Mag: 115mg

Ingredients

450g (1 lb) Lacinato Kale
75g (2.6 oz) Roasted Marcona Almonds
45ml (3 tbsp) Extra Virgin Olive Oil
10g (2 cloves) Fresh Garlic
15g (0.5 oz) Fresh Flat-leaf Parsley
1 large (60g) Organic Lemon
5g (1 tsp) Flaky Sea Salt
2g (0.5 tsp) Crushed Red Pepper Flakes
30g (1 oz) Shaved Parmesan Cheese

Step 1–3

Step 1: Clean and Dry

Thoroughly rinse 450g (1 lb) of Lacinato kale and dry completely using a salad spinner or clean towels to ensure a proper char.

Step 2: Stem Removal

Carefully strip the dark leaves away from the tough, woody central ribs and discard the stems.

Step 3: Leaf Preparation

Tear the kale leaves into bite-sized pieces, approximately 5cm (2 inches) in diameter.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Almond Crushing

Place 75g (2.6 oz) of Marcona almonds in a mortar or on a board and coarsely crush them to maintain a chunky texture.

Step 5: Gremolata Base

Finely mince 15g (0.5 oz) of parsley and 10g (2 cloves) of garlic until they form a fragrant, sandy texture.

Step 6: Citrus Zesting

Grate the zest of 1 lemon (60g) directly into the herb mixture and stir to combine the aromatic oils.

Step 7–9

Step 7: High Heat Searing

Heat a cast-iron skillet to 230°C (450°F) with 15ml (1 tbsp) of oil until shimmering, then add kale in batches.

Step 8: Infusion

Remove the charred kale from the heat and immediately toss with the remaining olive oil and the fresh gremolata mixture.

Step 9: Final Assembly

Fold in the crushed almonds, 30g (1 oz) of Parmesan, and season with 5g (1 tsp) of flaky salt and red pepper flakes.

Heirloom White Bean Velouté with Smoked Paprika Oil

"A silken embrace of earth and smoke, this velouté transforms humble heirloom white beans into a liquid velvet masterpiece, punctuated by the crimson fire of infused paprika oil and the subtle whisper of fresh thyme."

Prep Time20m
Cook Time50m
DifficultyMedium
Servings4
Nutrition380 kcal • Pro: 16g | Carbs: 42g | Fat: 15g | Ca: 195mg | Vit D: 2.5mcg | Mag: 135mg

Ingredients

400g (14 oz) Dried Heirloom White Beans, soaked overnight
1.2L (5 cups) Low-sodium vegetable stock
120ml (4 fl oz) Heavy cream
2 large Shallots, finely minced
3 cloves Garlic, smashed and peeled
60ml (2 fl oz) Extra virgin olive oil
5g (1 tsp) Smoked Spanish paprika (Pimentón)
2 sprigs Fresh thyme
1 Bay leaf
15ml (1 tbsp) Fresh lemon juice

Step 1–3

Step 1: Bean Preparation

Drain and rinse the 400g (14 oz) of soaked heirloom beans thoroughly in cold water to remove excess starches.

Step 2: Aromatic Base

In a heavy-bottomed pot, heat 15ml (1 tbsp) of olive oil over medium heat and sauté the minced shallots and garlic until translucent and fragrant.

Step 3: Simmering Process

Add the beans, 1.2L (5 cups) of stock, thyme, and the bay leaf; bring to a boil, then reduce heat to 90°C (194°F) and simmer for 45 minutes.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Infusing Paprika Oil

While the soup simmers, gently warm the remaining 45ml (1.5 fl oz) of oil with the 5g (1 tsp) of smoked paprika in a small pan for 5 minutes, then strain.

Step 5: Tenderness Check

Test the beans for a creamy consistency; they should mash easily against the side of the pot with no resistance at the core.

Step 6: Primary Blending

Remove the thyme sprigs and bay leaf, then use a high-speed immersion blender to process the soup until it reaches a thick, uniform consistency.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Refined Filtration

Pass the blended velouté through a fine-mesh chinois or sieve into a clean pot to ensure a perfectly smooth, professional texture.

Step 8: Final Seasoning

Stir in the 120ml (4 fl oz) of heavy cream and 15ml (1 tbsp) of lemon juice, heating gently to 70°C (158°F).

Step 9: Plating and Garnish

Ladle the velouté into warm bowls and drizzle the vibrant red smoked paprika oil over the surface in a decorative pattern.

Miso-Glazed King Oyster Mushrooms with Wilted Baby Bok Choy

"An umami-rich journey through Japanese-inspired flavors, featuring meaty king oyster mushrooms seared to golden perfection and lacquered in a savory-sweet miso glaze, nestled atop a bed of emerald, ginger-infused baby bok choy."

Prep Time20m
Cook Time25m
DifficultyMedium
Servings4
Nutrition320 kcal • Pro: 12g | Carbs: 28g | Fat: 18g | Ca: 180mg | Vit D: 8mcg | Mag: 95mg

Ingredients

450g (1 lb) King Oyster Mushrooms
400g (14 oz) Baby Bok Choy
45ml (3 tbsp) White Miso Paste
30ml (2 tbsp) Mirin
15ml (1 tbsp) Toasted Sesame Oil
10g (2 tsp) Fresh Ginger, minced
2 cloves (2 cloves) Garlic, minced
15ml (1 tbsp) Maple Syrup
30ml (2 tbsp) Neutral Vegetable Oil

Step 1–3

Step 1: Prepare the Mushrooms

Clean the king oyster mushrooms with a damp cloth and slice them in half lengthwise. Use a sharp knife to score a shallow diamond pattern into the flat, cut surface of each mushroom.

Step 2: Mix the Miso Glaze

In a small bowl, whisk together the white miso paste, mirin, maple syrup, and half of the minced ginger until the mixture is smooth and combined.

Step 3: Wash the Bok Choy

Slice the baby bok choy in half lengthwise. Rinse thoroughly under cold running water to remove any grit between the leaves, then pat dry with a clean towel.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Sear the Mushrooms

Heat 15ml (1 tbsp) of neutral oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Place the mushrooms cut-side down and sear for 4 to 5 minutes until deeply golden brown.

Step 5: Cook the Flip Side

Flip the mushrooms over and cook for another 3 to 4 minutes until they are tender throughout and the edges are slightly crisped.

Step 6: Caramelize with Glaze

Reduce the heat to low. Brush the miso glaze generously over the scored side of the mushrooms. Flip them back over for 30 seconds to caramelize the glaze, being careful not to let it burn.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Sauté the Aromatics

Remove mushrooms from the pan and set aside. In the same pan, add the remaining oil, garlic, and the rest of the ginger. Sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.

Step 8: Wilt the Bok Choy

Add the bok choy to the skillet with a splash of water. Cover with a lid and steam for 2 to 3 minutes until the stalks are tender-crisp and the leaves are vibrant green.

Step 9: Plate and Garnish

Arrange the wilted bok choy on a platter. Top with the glazed mushrooms and drizzle with toasted sesame oil before serving immediately.

Saffron-Infused Quinoa and Amaranth Risotto with Foraged Fungi

"A golden tapestry of ancient grains meets the earthy, umami-rich depths of the forest floor, where delicate saffron threads weave a sun-kissed aroma through a velvety emulsion of quinoa and amaranth. This dish reimagines the classic Italian technique using nutrient-dense pseudocereals and foraged treasures for a texture that is simultaneously crunchy, creamy, and profoundly savory."

Prep Time20m
Cook Time35m
DifficultyMedium
Servings4
Nutrition385 kcal • Pro: 14g | Carbs: 52g | Fat: 16g | Ca: 185mg | Vit D: 8mcg | Mag: 215mg

Ingredients

150g (5.3 oz) White Quinoa, rinsed
100g (3.5 oz) Amaranth
350g (12.3 oz) Foraged Mushrooms such as Chanterelles or Oyster
1L (4.2 cups) Low-sodium Vegetable Broth
0.5g (1 small pinch) Saffron Threads
120ml (0.5 cup) Dry White Wine
2 large Shallots, finely minced
3 cloves Garlic, minced
45g (3 tbsp) Grass-fed Butter or Vegan Butter
30ml (2 tbsp) Extra Virgin Olive Oil
5g (1 tsp) Fresh Thyme leaves
40g (1.4 oz) Grated Parmigiano-Reggiano or Nutritional Yeast

Step 1–3

Step 1: Infuse the Broth

Bring the vegetable broth to a gentle simmer in a medium saucepan. Add the saffron threads, turn the heat to low, and let the broth steep until it turns a vibrant golden-orange.

Step 2: Prepare the Fungi

Clean the foraged mushrooms with a soft brush. Slice the larger specimens into bite-sized pieces, leaving smaller ones whole to preserve their natural, rustic aesthetic.

Step 3: Sauté Aromatics

In a wide, heavy-bottomed skillet, heat 15ml (1 tbsp) of olive oil and 15g (1 tbsp) of butter. Sauté the shallots and garlic over medium heat for 3 minutes until translucent.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Toast the Grains

Add the quinoa and amaranth to the skillet. Stir constantly for 2 to 3 minutes to toast the grains until they emit a nutty aroma and are well-coated in the fat.

Step 5: Deglaze the Pan

Pour in the white wine, scraping the bottom of the pan to release any browned bits. Simmer until the liquid has been almost entirely absorbed by the grains.

Step 6: Gradual Hydration

Add the saffron-infused broth one ladleful at a time. Stir frequently and allow each addition to be absorbed before adding the next, continuing for about 20 to 25 minutes until the grains are tender yet firm.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Sear the Mushrooms

While the grains cook, heat the remaining oil and butter in a separate skillet over high heat. Sear the mushrooms until they are golden brown and crispy on the edges, seasoning with salt and thyme.

Step 8: Emulsify and Finish

Stir the remaining butter and the grated cheese or nutritional yeast into the grain mixture. Fold in half of the seared mushrooms to incorporate their flavor throughout the risotto.

Step 9: Garnish and Serve

Divide the risotto into warm bowls. Top with the remaining crispy mushrooms, a sprinkle of fresh thyme, and a final crack of black pepper.

Braised Tempeh Medallions in a Mission Fig and Balsamic Reduction

"Experience a sophisticated marriage of textures where the firm, nutty bite of fermented tempeh meets the jammy, honeyed depth of Mission figs. This dish is elevated by a velvety balsamic reduction that balances bright acidity with a lingering, woodsy rosemary finish, creating a plant-based centerpiece that rivals any traditional roast."

Prep Time15m
Cook Time45m
DifficultyMedium
Servings4
Nutrition450 kcal • Pro: 24g | Carbs: 48g | Fat: 19g | Ca: 185mg | Vit D: 4mcg | Mag: 115mg

Ingredients

400g (14 oz) Organic Tempeh
150g (5.3 oz) Dried Mission Figs, halved
60ml (2 fl oz) High-quality Balsamic Vinegar
240ml (8 fl oz) Vegetable Broth
30ml (2 tbsp) Grade A Maple Syrup
30ml (2 tbsp) Extra Virgin Olive Oil
50g (1.8 oz) Shallots, finely minced
3 cloves (15g) Garlic, thinly sliced
1 sprig (2g) Fresh Rosemary

Step 1–3

Step 1: Prepare Medallions

Slice the 400g (14 oz) tempeh block into uniform medallions approximately 1cm (0.4 in) thick for even cooking.

Step 2: Sear the Tempeh

Heat 30ml (2 tbsp) olive oil in a heavy skillet to 175°C (350°F) and sear medallions for 4 minutes per side until golden brown.

Step 3: Sauté Aromatics

Reduce heat slightly and add 50g (1.8 oz) shallots and 3 garlic cloves, cooking for 3 minutes until translucent and fragrant.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Prepare the Figs

Add the 150g (5.3 oz) halved Mission figs to the pan, allowing them to slightly soften in the residual heat.

Step 5: Deglaze the Pan

Pour in 60ml (2 fl oz) balsamic vinegar and 30ml (2 tbsp) maple syrup, scraping the bottom of the pan to release the fond.

Step 6: Introduce Braising Liquid

Add 240ml (8 fl oz) vegetable broth and the rosemary sprig, ensuring the medallions are partially submerged.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Simmer and Reduce

Bring to a gentle simmer at 95°C (200°F) and cook for 15-20 minutes until the liquid reduces by two-thirds.

Step 8: Glaze the Medallions

Use a spoon to baste the tempeh medallions with the thickening balsamic reduction until they are glossy and coated.

Step 9: Final Resting

Remove from heat and let the dish rest for 5 minutes to allow the sauce to set to a syrupy consistency before serving.

Roasted Cauliflower Steaks with Caper and Golden Raisin Agrodolce

"Experience a masterclass in Mediterranean contrast with these thick-cut cauliflower slabs, roasted until their edges achieve a deep, nutty caramelization. Drenched in a shimmering Sicilian agrodolce, the dish balances the briny punch of capers against the honeyed sweetness of golden raisins, creating a vibrant symphony of flavors that elevates the humble brassica to center-stage status."

Prep Time15m
Cook Time35m
DifficultyMedium
Servings4
Nutrition310 kcal • Pro: 7g | Carbs: 34g | Fat: 19g | Ca: 112mg | Vit D: 0mcg | Mag: 68mg

Ingredients

2 large heads (approx. 1.8 kg) Cauliflower
60 ml (4 tbsp) Extra virgin olive oil
5 g (1 tsp) Kosher salt
2 g (1/2 tsp) Cracked black pepper
80 g (1/2 cup) Golden raisins
45 g (3 tbsp) Non-pareil capers, rinsed
60 ml (1/4 cup) White balsamic vinegar
20 g (1 tbsp) Wildflower honey
30 g (1/4 cup) Toasted pine nuts
15 g (1/2 cup) Fresh Italian parsley, chopped

Step 1–3

Step 1: Oven Calibration

Preheat your oven to 220°C (425°F) and position two heavy-rimmed baking sheets inside to heat up, ensuring a superior sear on the vegetables.

Step 2: Carving the Steaks

Remove the outer leaves of the cauliflower. Slice each head vertically through the core into 2.5 cm (1 inch) thick 'steaks', reserving any loose florets for another use.

Step 3: Infusing with Oil

Generously brush both sides of the steaks with 45 ml (3 tbsp) of olive oil, then season evenly with salt and pepper.

Step 4–6

Step 4: The Initial Sear

Carefully place the steaks onto the preheated baking sheets. Roast for 15 minutes (15m) until the bottom surfaces are deeply browned.

Step 5: The Careful Flip

Using a wide spatula, gently flip the steaks over. Return to the oven and roast for another 10 to 15 minutes (10-15m) until tender throughout.

Step 6: Reducing the Agrodolce

In a small saucepan over medium heat, combine the white balsamic vinegar and honey. Simmer for 3 minutes (3m) until the liquid reduces slightly into a light syrup.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Building the Flavor Profile

Stir the golden raisins, capers, and the remaining 15 ml (1 tbsp) of olive oil into the vinegar reduction. Warm through for 2 minutes (2m) to plump the raisins.

Step 8: The Final Assembly

Transfer the hot cauliflower steaks to a warm serving platter. Spoon the warm raisin and caper agrodolce generously over the center of each steak.

Step 9: Garnish and Serve

Scatter the toasted pine nuts and chopped parsley over the dish. Serve immediately while the temperatures and textures are at their peak.

Chickpea and Spinach Cassoulet with a Herbed Sourdough Crust

"A rustic, plant-forward twist on a French classic, this cassoulet replaces heavy meats with buttery chickpeas and vibrant spinach, all tucked beneath a golden, jagged crown of herb-infused sourdough crumbles that shatter with every spoonful. The earthy depth of smoked paprika and thyme creates a savory foundation that warms the soul without the weight of traditional stews."

Prep Time20m
Cook Time40m
DifficultyMedia
Servings4
Nutrition380 kcal • Pro: 16g | Carbs: 52g | Fat: 14g | Ca: 180mg | Vit D: 0mcg | Mag: 145mg

Ingredients

800g (28 oz) Canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed
250g (8.8 oz) Fresh baby spinach
150g (5.3 oz) Sourdough bread, torn into bite-sized chunks
500ml (17 fl oz) Vegetable stock
2 cloves (0.2 oz) Garlic, finely minced
1 large (150g / 5.3 oz) Yellow onion, finely diced
30ml (2 tbsp) Extra virgin olive oil
5g (1 tsp) Dried thyme
2g (0.5 tsp) Smoked paprika
30g (1 oz) Fresh flat-leaf parsley, chopped

Step 1–3

Step 1: Oven and Dish Preparation

Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F) and lightly grease a medium-sized ceramic baking dish with a small amount of oil.

Step 2: Sauté the Aromatics

In a large skillet, heat 15ml (1 tbsp) of olive oil over medium heat, then sauté the onion and garlic for 5 minutes until translucent.

Step 3: Toast the Spices

Stir in the dried thyme and smoked paprika, cooking for 1 minute until the spices become fragrant and the oil turns a deep red hue.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Simmer the Base

Pour in the 500ml (17 fl oz) of vegetable stock and bring the liquid to a gentle simmer, scraping the bottom of the pan to release flavor.

Step 5: Incorporate Chickpeas

Add the 800g (28 oz) of chickpeas to the skillet, stirring well to coat them in the spiced broth and simmer for 5 minutes.

Step 6: Wilt the Spinach

Add the 250g (8.8 oz) of spinach in batches, stirring gently until the leaves have fully wilted into the chickpea mixture.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Prepare the Sourdough Crust

In a bowl, toss the sourdough chunks with the remaining 15ml (1 tbsp) of oil and the chopped parsley until evenly coated.

Step 8: Assemble the Cassoulet

Transfer the chickpea and spinach mixture into the prepared baking dish, then scatter the herbed sourdough chunks evenly over the top.

Step 9: Bake to Golden Perfection

Bake for 20 minutes at 200°C (400°F) until the sourdough crust is deeply golden-brown and the stew is bubbling at the edges.

Slow-Roasted Heirloom Carrots with Pistachio Dukkah and Mint

"A tapestry of earth-born colors meets the crunch of the Levantine desert in this vibrant celebration of root vegetables. These heirloom carrots are coaxed into a state of tender caramelization through a gentle, slow roast, then adorned with a hand-crushed pistachio Dukkah that hums with toasted cumin and coriander, all brightened by a cooling whisper of garden-fresh mint."

Prep Time15m
Cook Time45m
DifficultyMedia
Servings4
Nutrition295 kcal • Pro: 5g | Carbs: 32g | Fat: 16g | Ca: 145mg | Vit D: 0mcg | Mag: 92mg

Ingredients

800g (1.75 lbs) Heirloom Carrots, cleaned and tops trimmed
45ml (3 tbsp) Extra Virgin Olive Oil
30ml (2 tbsp) Wildflower Honey
60g (2.1 oz) Raw Shelled Pistachios
20g (0.7 oz) White Sesame Seeds
5g (1 tsp) Coriander Seeds
5g (1 tsp) Cumin Seeds
10g (0.35 oz) Fresh Mint Leaves, chiffonade
5ml (1 tsp) Lemon Juice
2g (0.5 tsp) Flaky Sea Salt

Step 1–3

Step 1: Oven Calibration

Preheat your oven to 160°C (320°F) and line a large baking sheet with parchment paper to prevent sticking.

Step 2: Seasoning the Roots

Place the cleaned heirloom carrots on the tray and toss with 30ml (2 tbsp) of olive oil and a pinch of salt until evenly coated.

Step 3: The Initial Roast

Arrange the carrots in a single layer and roast for 30 minutes, allowing the natural sugars to concentrate without charring the skin.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Toasting the Aromatics

While the carrots roast, place the pistachios, sesame seeds, coriander, and cumin in a dry skillet over medium heat for 3-5 minutes until fragrant.

Step 5: Crafting the Dukkah

Transfer the toasted nut and spice mixture to a mortar and pestle; crush into a coarse, gravel-like texture rather than a fine powder.

Step 6: Preparing the Glaze

In a small ramekin, whisk together the wildflower honey and the remaining 15ml (1 tbsp) of olive oil until emulsified.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Glazing the Carrots

Remove the carrots from the oven and brush them generously with the honey-oil mixture, turning them to ensure full coverage.

Step 8: Final Caramelization

Return the carrots to the oven for a final 15 minutes at 180°C (350°F) to achieve a sticky, golden exterior.

Step 9: Plating and Garnish

Arrange the warm carrots on a platter, sprinkle heavily with the pistachio Dukkah, fresh mint, and a squeeze of lemon juice.

Buckwheat Galettes with Savory Leek and Cashew Cream Filling

"An earthy, rustic journey into the heart of Brittany, reimagined with a velvety plant-based core. These nutty, lace-edged buckwheat crepes embrace a decadent fusion of melt-in-your-mouth leeks and a probiotic-rich cashew emulsion, offering a sophisticated harmony of textures and deep, savory satisfaction."

Prep Time15m
Cook Time45m
DifficultyMedia
Servings4
Nutrition420 kcal • Pro: 12g | Carbs: 48g | Fat: 22g | Ca: 85mg | Vit D: 0mcg | Mag: 145mg

Ingredients

250g (8.8 oz) Buckwheat flour
500ml (2.1 cups) Cold water
150g (5.3 oz) Raw cashews, soaked for 4 hours
300g (10.6 oz) Fresh leeks, sliced into rounds
30ml (2 tbsp) Extra virgin olive oil
15ml (1 tbsp) Fresh lemon juice
10g (0.35 oz) Nutritional yeast
5g (1 tsp) Sea salt
2g (0.07 oz) Cracked black pepper

Step 1–3

Step 1: Hydrate the Flour

In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the buckwheat flour and 2g (0.4 tsp) of salt with the cold water until a smooth, thin batter forms.

Step 2: Emulsify the Cashew Cream

Drain the soaked cashews and place them in a blender with the lemon juice, nutritional yeast, and 60ml (2 fl oz) of fresh water, blending until perfectly smooth.

Step 3: Prepare the Leeks

Thoroughly wash the leeks and slice the white and light green parts into 5mm (0.2 in) thick rounds.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Sauté the Aromatics

Heat half the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat and sauté the leeks with the remaining salt and pepper for 10 minutes until soft and translucent.

Step 5: Integrate the Filling

Stir the prepared cashew cream into the sautéed leeks, cooking for another 2 minutes until the mixture is thick and binds together.

Step 6: Temper the Skillet

Heat a flat, non-stick crepe pan or galetière to 200°C (400°F) and lightly grease with the remaining olive oil.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Cast the Galette

Ladle 120ml (0.5 cup) of batter into the center of the pan, quickly swirling it to create a thin, even circle.

Step 8: Assemble the Parcel

Spread a quarter of the leek filling in the center of the galette, then fold the four edges inward to create a square shape, leaving the center exposed.

Step 9: Finish and Plate

Cook for 2 more minutes until the base is crisp, then slide onto a plate and garnish with fresh chives or microgreens.

Warm Puy Lentil and Walnut Salad with Pomegranate Gastrique

"This sophisticated ensemble marries the peppery, resilient bite of French Puy lentils with the buttery crunch of oven-toasted walnuts, all brought to life by a vibrant, ruby-hued pomegranate gastrique. A masterclass in balancing earthy depths with sharp, acidic brightness, it transforms humble legumes into a centerpiece of high-culinary art."

Prep Time15m
Cook Time45m
DifficultyMedia
Servings4
Nutrition415 kcal • Pro: 14g | Carbs: 45g | Fat: 22g | Ca: 120mg | Vit D: 0mcg | Mag: 145mg

Ingredients

250g (8.8 oz) dried Puy lentils
750ml (3.2 cups) water
100g (3.5 oz) walnut halves
120ml (4 fl oz) pomegranate juice
30ml (2 tbsp) red wine vinegar
30ml (2 tbsp) honey
60ml (4 tbsp) extra virgin olive oil
50g (1.7 oz) fresh pomegranate arils
30g (1 oz) fresh flat-leaf parsley, chopped
1 medium shallot, minced
1 bay leaf

Step 1–3

Step 1: Rinse and Prepare

Place 250g (8.8 oz) of Puy lentils in a fine-mesh sieve and rinse thoroughly under cold running water to remove any dust or debris.

Step 2: Simmer the Lentils

Transfer the lentils to a medium pot with 750ml (3.2 cups) of water and a bay leaf; bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 20-25 minutes until tender but firm.

Step 3: Toast the Walnuts

Preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F) and toast 100g (3.5 oz) of walnut halves on a baking sheet for 8 minutes until fragrant and golden.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Prepare Gastrique Base

In a small heavy-bottomed saucepan, combine 120ml (4 fl oz) pomegranate juice, 30ml (2 tbsp) red wine vinegar, and 30ml (2 tbsp) honey.

Step 5: Reduce to Syrup

Simmer the pomegranate mixture over medium-high heat for approximately 10-12 minutes until it reduces by half and coats the back of a spoon.

Step 6: Mince Fresh Aromatics

While the sauce reduces, finely mince the shallot and chop 30g (1 oz) of fresh parsley to ensure a uniform distribution of flavor.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Drain and Temper

Drain the cooked lentils through a sieve, discarding the bay leaf, and let them sit for 2 minutes to allow excess steam to escape.

Step 8: Emulsify the Dressing

Slowly whisk 60ml (4 tbsp) of extra virgin olive oil into the warm pomegranate gastrique until a smooth, slightly thickened emulsion forms.

Step 9: Assemble and Garnish

In a large bowl, toss the warm lentils with the walnuts, shallots, and parsley; drizzle with the gastrique and top with 50g (1.7 oz) pomegranate arils.

Chilled Avocado and Cucumber Gazpacho with Toasted Hemp Hearts

"This chilled masterpiece is a symphony of botanical freshness, where the velvet embrace of ripe avocados meets the crisp, hydrating soul of garden cucumbers. A sophisticated starter that sparkles with lime zest and the earthy, toasted crunch of nutrient-dense hemp hearts, providing a cooling sanctuary on a warm afternoon."

Prep Time15m
Cook Time5m
DifficultyEasy
Servings4
Nutrition350 kcal • Pro: 8g | Carbs: 12g | Fat: 31g | Ca: 75mg | Vit D: 0mcg | Mag: 155mg

Ingredients

500g (1.1 lbs) English Cucumbers
3 large (3 large) Ripe Avocados
250ml (8.5 fl oz) Cold Vegetable Broth
45ml (3 tbsp) Fresh Lime Juice
20g (0.7 oz) Fresh Cilantro
2 cloves (2 cloves) Garlic
60g (2.1 oz) Raw Hemp Hearts
30ml (2 tbsp) Extra Virgin Olive Oil
3g (0.6 tsp) Sea Salt

Step 1–3

Step 1: Produce Preparation

Wash the 500g (1.1 lbs) of cucumbers, peel them roughly, and chop into large chunks to prepare for blending.

Step 2: Avocado Extraction

Halve the 3 large (3 large) avocados, remove the pits, and scoop the creamy flesh directly into a high-speed blender.

Step 3: Aromatic Foundation

Add 2 cloves (2 cloves) of peeled garlic and 20g (0.7 oz) of fresh cilantro to the blender jar for a punch of aromatics.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Hydration Phase

Pour 250ml (8.5 fl oz) of chilled vegetable broth over the ingredients to provide the necessary liquid base for the soup.

Step 5: Acid and Seasoning

Incorporate 45ml (3 tbsp) of fresh lime juice and 3g (0.6 tsp) of sea salt to balance the richness of the healthy fats.

Step 6: The Emulsion

Blend the mixture on high speed for approximately 60 seconds until the gazpacho is completely smooth and reaches a vibrant emerald hue.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Thermal Setting

Transfer the soup to a glass container and chill in the refrigerator at 4°C (40°F) for at least 30 minutes to develop the flavors.

Step 8: The Toasting

In a dry skillet over medium heat at 175°C (350°F), toast 60g (2.1 oz) of hemp hearts until golden and fragrant.

Step 9: Artistic Assembly

Ladle the soup into bowls, drizzle with 30ml (2 tbsp) of olive oil, and garnish generously with the toasted hemp hearts.

Compressed Watermelon and Radish Carpaccio with Tahini-Lime Emulsion

"A vibrant, avant-garde exploration of textures where the osmotic pressure of a vacuum seal transforms humble watermelon into a dense, sashimi-like ruby gem, balanced by the peppery snap of radishes and a velvety, citrus-forward tahini lace."

Prep Time30m
Cook Time1h
DifficultyMedia
Servings4
Nutrition240 kcal • Pro: 5g | Carbs: 25g | Fat: 15g | Ca: 140mg | Vit D: 0mcg | Mag: 90mg

Ingredients

800g (1.75 lbs) Seedless Watermelon
150g (5.3 oz) Watermelon Radish
60ml (4 tbsp) Raw Tahini
30ml (2 tbsp) Fresh Lime Juice
15ml (1 tbsp) Maple Syrup
2g (0.4 tsp) Flaky Sea Salt
10g (0.35 oz) Fresh Mint Leaves
5g (1 tsp) Black Sesame Seeds
45ml (3 tbsp) Extra Virgin Olive Oil

Step 1–3

Step 1: Watermelon Fabrication

Slice the seedless watermelon into uniform rectangular planks approximately 2cm (0.8 in) thick, ensuring all rind and white pith are removed.

Step 2: Vacuum Compression

Place the watermelon planks into high-quality vacuum-seal bags in a single layer and seal at the highest pressure setting to collapse the cellular structure.

Step 3: Thermal Stabilization

Transfer the sealed bags to the refrigerator and allow them to chill at 4°C (39°F) for at least 45 minutes to set the dense, meaty texture.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Precision Mandolin Work

Using a Japanese mandolin, slice the watermelon radishes into paper-thin rounds roughly 1mm (0.04 in) thick, then submerge in ice water to maintain crispness.

Step 5: Tahini Base Integration

In a small glass bowl, whisk together the raw tahini, fresh lime juice, and maple syrup until the mixture forms a thick, paste-like consistency.

Step 6: Emulsion Refinement

Gradually whisk in 30ml (2 tbsp) of ice-cold water into the tahini base until the emulsion becomes pale, smooth, and easily pourable.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Carpaccio Sheeting

Remove the watermelon from the vacuum bags and slice the dense planks into translucent 3mm (0.12 in) sheets using a very sharp sashimi knife.

Step 8: Geometric Plating

On chilled ceramic plates, alternate the watermelon and radish slices in an overlapping circular pattern to create a striking mosaic effect.

Step 9: Final Aromatic Garnish

Drizzle with the tahini-lime emulsion and olive oil, then scatter black sesame seeds, flaky salt, and hand-torn mint over the top.

Toasted Pine Nut and Herb-Crusted Tofu with a Parsley Coulis

"Imagine a silken block of premium soy transformed into a culinary masterpiece, where the earthy crunch of golden-toasted pine nuts meets the vibrant, emerald essence of a garden-fresh parsley coulis. This dish balances the delicate texture of pressed tofu with a sophisticated, nutty exterior, finished with a bright citrus-infused herb reduction that dances across the palate."

Prep Time20m
Cook Time25m
DifficultyMedia
Servings4
Nutrition410 kcal • Pro: 22g | Carbs: 15g | Fat: 32g | Ca: 380mg | Vit D: 4mcg | Mag: 140mg

Ingredients

700g (24.7 oz) Extra-firm tofu
80g (2.8 oz) Raw pine nuts
40g (1.4 oz) Fresh flat-leaf parsley
30g (1 oz) Panko breadcrumbs
30ml (2 tbsp) Dijon mustard
60ml (4 tbsp) Extra virgin olive oil
15ml (1 tbsp) Fresh lemon juice
2g (1/2 tsp) Sea salt
1g (1/4 tsp) Cracked black pepper

Step 1–3

Step 1: Press the Tofu

Wrap the extra-firm tofu in a clean kitchen towel and place a heavy skillet or weight on top for 20 minutes to remove excess moisture.

Step 2: Toast the Pine Nuts

Place the pine nuts in a dry pan over medium heat, stirring constantly for 3 minutes (180 seconds) until they are golden brown.

Step 3: Prepare the Herb Crust

In a food processor, pulse the toasted pine nuts, panko breadcrumbs, and half of the parsley until a coarse, uniform crumb forms.

Step 4–6

Step 4: Blend the Parsley Coulis

Combine the remaining parsley, lemon juice, and 30ml (2 tbsp) of olive oil in a high-speed blender until completely smooth.

Step 5: Slice the Tofu

Cut the pressed tofu into four even rectangular steaks, approximately 2cm (0.8 in) thick, and pat dry with a paper towel.

Step 6: Coat with Binder

Brush all sides of each tofu steak with a thin, even layer of Dijon mustard to serve as the adhesive for the crust.

Step 7–9

Step 7: Apply the Nut Crust

Press each mustard-coated tofu steak firmly into the pine nut and herb mixture, ensuring every side is heavily encrusted.

Step 8: Sear the Steaks

Heat the remaining oil in a non-stick skillet over medium-high heat and sear the tofu for 4 minutes (240 seconds) per side until crispy.

Step 9: Plate and Garnish

Drizzle a generous circle of parsley coulis on a plate, place the tofu steak in the center, and season with sea salt and pepper.
